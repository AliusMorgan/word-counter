About Us

Word Counter is a free tool for writers, students, translators, marketers and anyone who needs quick, accurate text statistics. We built it to be fast, private and simple, with full Arabic and English support.

Our principles: your text stays on your device, there is nothing to install and no account to create, and the tool works the same on phones, tablets and computers.

We keep improving the tool based on user feedback. Have an idea or found a problem? Visit the Contact page.
